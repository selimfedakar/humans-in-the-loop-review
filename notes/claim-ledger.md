## Confidence levels

- **A** — primary source read directly, claim is explicit in it.
- **B** — secondary source / stated by a reliable summary, not yet read in primary.
- **C** — inferred or extrapolated; flagged as the author's interpretation.
- **D** — contested; sources disagree. Present both sides.
